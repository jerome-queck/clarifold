## Candidate prompts and construction notes

### Mirror

Prompt intent: a premium geometric, vector-like 3D standalone mark based on two opposing curved geometric planes facing across a narrow luminous seam. The desired interpretation was reflective examination of understanding, with indigo, midnight blue, muted violet, cool silver, and one restrained warm highlight.

The prompt required a centered square mark with generous padding, strong silhouette, and no text or watermark. It explicitly excluded graduation caps, brains, robots, AI circuitry, pi symbols, equations, generic checkmarks, trophies, medals, lightbulbs, books, rockets, people, eyes, clutter, and imagery implying guaranteed mastery or correctness.

### Proof structure

Prompt intent: a compact arrangement of interlocking geometric supports around a deliberate central opening, suggesting claims supported by dependencies rather than a success symbol. The desired treatment was calm, exact, architectural, and minimally detailed.

The prompt required the opening and support relationships to remain meaningful without color. It explicitly excluded education and AI clichés, equations, formula text, generic checkmarks, trophies, medals, clutter, and any promise of guaranteed mastery or correctness.

### Learning trail

Prompt intent: one continuous geometric path with three or four substantial waypoints that turns inward toward a quiet central gap, suggesting visible and revisitable progress. The mark was specified as a designed emblem, not a map, arrow, timeline, or generic progress indicator.

The prompt required a centered, compact silhouette with indigo, blue-violet, cool silver, restrained teal, and one small amber highlight. It explicitly excluded arrowheads, map pins, chevrons, graduation caps, brains, robots, equations, generic checkmarks, and mastery claims.

### Unfolding surface

Prompt intent: a mathematically clean folded surface opening into broad connected planes around a small illuminated gap, suggesting clarity emerging as structure is unfolded and inspected. The desired treatment was lucid, curious, precise, and quietly optimistic.

The prompt required the gap to communicate an inspectable question rather than completion. It explicitly excluded literal origami cranes, flowers, graduation caps, brains, robots, equations, generic checkmarks, clutter, and imagery implying guaranteed mastery or correctness.

| Review size | Intended check |
| ---: | --- |
| 1024 px | large candidate review and future macOS source-size discussion |
| 512 px | large Finder/Dock-style review |
| 256 px | medium icon review |

| 64 px | compact menu and utility review |
| 32 px | small toolbar-style review |
| 16 px | minimum legibility check |

The context board places each source candidate on dark and light surrounding surfaces and includes explicit 360 px, 128 px, and 32 px previews on both surfaces. Its rounded-corner mask removes only generated corner fill for presentation; it is a review aid, not a signed product screenshot, application resource, or release artifact.
